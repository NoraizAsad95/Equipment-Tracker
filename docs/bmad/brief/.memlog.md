# Memlog: Equipment-Tracker brief

topic: Equipment-Tracker (Horizon Survey Services)
created: 2026-10-21

- [event] Run started, Create intent, Coaching path. Context: hiring assessment from Solvefy; user is the candidate.
- [decision] Task document (owner voice note + follow-up email) is the source of truth. Do not invent requirements; present options and trade-offs, user decides.
- [decision] Stack constraint from task: .NET 8/9 ASP.NET Core Web API, C#, relational DB (SQLite/LocalDB), free choice of frontend. Data model quality scored above UI polish.
- [decision] D1: Single Equipment concept with TrackingMode (Individual vs Quantity). Individual: specific item, current holder, checkout/return dates, due date, condition, history. Quantity: track counts only (no invented identities, per "not individually tracked"); transaction history records quantity out/in and responsible person. Reason: preserves client's distinction, one consistent model, one checkout system.
- [open] D1 consequence: how does a damaged return work for Quantity items (no identity)? Parked to the damage decision.
- [decision] D2: No kit entity or template (task never defines kit contents, fixed-ness, or identity). "Whole kit" = multi-item selection in one checkout transaction. Each Individual item keeps its own checkout/return record. Partial kit return: returned items are closed, missing Individual item stays checked out to the same person; transaction closes only when all Individual items are back. Reason: no invented scope; missing item stays visible.
- [open] D2 tension: multi-select every time vs "few seconds" speed requirement. Mitigation must come from UI design, not new scope.
- [decision] D3 (provisional): Configurable approval rule (not hard-coded). Mechanism: request stays pending until an authorized approver approves/rejects; equipment is not checked out before approval. Record request, decision, approver, decision time. Drone requires approval with Hamza as approver; others direct unless enabled later. No notification system or complex workflow for the assessment.
- [open] D3: user said "per equipment type" but task says "the new rover" (one of two rovers) and "the drone"; granularity (type vs individual item) unresolved. Also unresolved: is item held while a request is pending, and what happens on reject.
- [decision] D3 (final): Approval setting lives on the individual item (so the new rover can be flagged without affecting the old one). Approver stored as a user reference (minimal user model defined in the identity decision). Pending request temporarily reserves the item (unavailable to other requests, not yet checked out); approval proceeds to checkout, rejection releases the hold; rejection reason optional. No notifications or extra workflow.
- [decision] D4: Borrower selects a required due date at checkout (no defaults). Overdue is computed and visible on screens only; no reminders or escalation (task does not ask for them). Counted items: one due date per checkout; a partial return leaves the remaining quantity outstanding and overdue after that date.
- [note] D4: user said the task asks for overdue to be visible on screen; strictly, the task asks for "back on time" and "see it on a screen" (who has what). Overdue visibility is our reading, not an explicit requirement. Keep as stated assumption in brief.
- [decision] D5 (provisional): Condition (OK/Damaged + optional note) required at return; last reported condition and note shown to the next borrower at checkout (warn only, no blocking). Counted items: record damaged quantity, removed from available quantity.
- [open] D5: inconsistency to resolve. Individual damaged items stay available with a warning, but damaged counted units leave availability. Also no way defined to restore damaged units (repair) to the pool.
- [change] D5 revised (final): Known-damaged equipment is NOT available for normal checkout. Individual items: unavailable until condition restored to OK. Counted items: track total vs usable quantity (e.g. 6 total, 5 usable, 1 damaged). Condition (OK/Damaged + optional note) still captured at return. No repair workflow. Supersedes the earlier "warn only" version; resolves the individual/counted inconsistency.
- [open] D5: who may restore a damaged item/unit to usable, and what a restore records. Depends on identity decision. Risk: a mistaken damage report locks equipment out.
- [decision] D6: Hybrid identity. Borrowing: pick name from list (no PIN). Approval and damage restoration require a short PIN. Saima may restore damaged equipment and handle day-to-day equipment records; Tariq may manage equipment, approval settings and users; other staff cannot. Saima and Tariq both have person/user records.
- [open] D6: "day-to-day equipment records" for Saima is undefined; overlap with Tariq's management. Accepted limitation: name-pick borrowing means a person could check out as someone else; accountability for borrowing is trust-based.
- [decision] D6 clarified: Saima restores damaged equipment only; Tariq manages equipment, approval settings, users.
- [decision] D7: Date-range period list of checkout/return records (borrower, equipment, checkout date, due date, return date); no point-in-time reconstruction. Records immutable; corrections recorded as separate records.
- [decision] Global constraint from user: keep it simple; only features justified by the task and logged decisions; no extra roles, abstractions, workflows, microservices; auth limited to name selection + PIN; minor unknowns recorded as assumptions, not questions.
- [assumption] See brief.md Assumptions section (approval for Individual only; history for Tariq+Saima read-only; no past due dates; no pending timeout; returns recorded by any picked name; Pakistan local time; seed data).
